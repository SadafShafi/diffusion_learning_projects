=====================================================================
 ALL EXPERIMENTS - copy/paste from the project folder
=====================================================================
Pattern:   sbatch [slurm options] train.sh <script.py> [script options]
  - train.sh = train + test in one job (--mode all)
  - run name: automatic <model>_<dataset>_<timestamp>  (or add --run_name NAME)
  - results:  outputs/<run_name>/   checkpoint: checkpoints/<run_name>.pt   log: logs/train_<jobid>.out
  - watch:    squeue -u $USER          tail -f logs/train_<jobid>.out
  - cancel:   scancel <jobid>

Partitions (check what's free: sinfo -p grete,grete:interactive -t mixed,idle -N -O "NodeList,Gres:45,GresUsed:60"):
  - grete              full A100, often a queue
  - grete:interactive  slices of an A100 (MIG), usually free, max 12h
        1g.10gb (1/7 A100), 2g.20gb (2/7), 3g.40gb (3/7)

0) ONCE, on the login node (internet needed)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
bash prepare.sh

# --- CIFAR-10 (100k steps, ~4.8M params). Needs a full A100: several hours
sbatch --time=08:00:00 train.sh ddpm_images_Sg1_2.py --dataset cifar10

# --- re-test a finished run with more FID samples (more reliable number)
# sbatch test.sh ddpm_images_Sg1_2.py --run_name <run_name> --n_fid 10000

=====================================================================
 STAGE 3.1 (project 6)  Latent diffusion on CelebA 128x128   (latent_Sg3_1.py)
=====================================================================
# 1) look at the VAE (CPU is fine):            python latent_Sg3_1.py --mode vae_check
# 2) encode the dataset once (~16 min):
sbatch -p grete:interactive --gpus=3g.40gb:1 --mem=48G --time=01:00:00 test.sh latent_Sg3_1.py --mode encode
# 3) train + test the latent U-Net:
sbatch --mem=48G --time=03:00:00 train.sh latent_Sg3_1.py
# pixel-space baseline with the same training time:
sbatch --mem=48G --time=03:00:00 train.sh ddpm_images_Sg1_2.py --method flow --dataset celeba --time_limit_min 70 --sample_batch 250
# memorisation + VAE-ceiling check:
sbatch -p grete:interactive --gpus=3g.40gb:1 --mem=48G --time=01:00:00 test.sh latent_Sg3_1.py --run_name <run> --mode sanity

What to look at in outputs/<run_name>/:
  progress_overview.png   how samples improve during training
  samples.png             real vs generated
  input_output_grid.png   noise a real image to t, denoise back
  input_output_steps.png  the steps in between
  x0_prediction.png       model's one-step guess of the clean image at each t
  reverse_process.png     pure noise -> image
  results.json            loss, FID, times, nearest-neighbour distances
  nearest_neighbours.png  model's guess during sampling + its closest TRAINING image
  nearest_final.png       final samples next to their closest training image
  identity_curve.png      P(same digit) vs t  (+ identity_examples.png, identity_curve.json)
